How DealPilot works

Data sources

  • Official retailer APIs
  • Affiliate feeds
  • Merchant feeds
  • Approved product-data providers

No scraping that violates retailer terms. Every price row must carry source, timestamp, currency, shipping and availability.

What is demo today

All products, offers, retailers and guides in this build are demo seed data. Price history is intentionally empty. Alerts are stored in your browser only. No affiliate URL is configured, so “View Deal” logs a local click instead of redirecting.

Price data is provided for comparison purposes. Availability and prices can change.
